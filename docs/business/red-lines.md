# Red lines — content & business (proposed 2026-10-08, owner to approve)

Things Great Hikes never does, whatever the money. They protect the community's trust (our main
asset), keep us inside the law (B6) and inside platform rules (Instagram, Google, affiliate
programmes). Once approved, they go into the constitution and the content policy (B5).

## Content
1. **No explicit content** — no nudity, sexual content, gore, hate, harassment, or shock imagery.
2. **No stolen work** — never publish a photo or text without the creator's permission and a
   visible credit; licensed stock only within its licence; takedown within 48 h on a valid claim.
3. **No fake visuals** — no AI-generated or AI-edited photos presented as real places (resizing only).
4. **No unsafe or illegal promotion** — no encouraging closed-area or off-trail hiking where
   banned, trespassing, drones where prohibited, feeding/approaching wildlife, dangerous stunts for
   photos; we follow Leave No Trace.
5. **Sensitive places** — don't publish exact locations that rangers or communities ask to keep
   private (fragile sites, sacred places, nesting areas); respect Indigenous/cultural sites' rules.
6. **Accuracy** — trip facts only from official sources, dated, with corrections when wrong; never
   invented numbers.
7. **No politics/divisive campaigning** on the brand channels (conservation facts are fine).

## Community
8. **No fake, bought or incentivised reviews**; no hiding honest negative reviews (only rule
   breakers are hidden).
9. **No personal data exposure** — no faces of minors without guardian consent, no private
   information in posts; members can delete their content anytime.

## Money
10. **No paid placement inside guides, rankings or the top 10** — partners can sponsor clearly
    labelled sections, never buy a position.
11. **Always label** affiliate links, sponsored content and partner ads.
12. **Only recommend products we would use**; no affiliate deals with brands that conflict with
    the outdoors (e.g. destructive off-road tourism).
13. **No selling or renting personal data**; no tracking without consent.
14. **No dark patterns** — no fake urgency, hidden costs, forced sign-ups to read content.
