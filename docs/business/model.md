# Great Hikes — business model (B1 draft v1, 2026-10-08)

All numbers are **assumptions to validate**, not forecasts. Update them in the learning log as real
data arrives.

## Revenue lines (in the order we switch them on)
| # | Line | When | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Gear affiliates** (AvantLink: Backcountry, Osprey, Black Diamond…; Amazon later) | After launch, once approved | 4–10 % commission, 30–60-day cookies; best inside guides ("what to pack for the W Trek") |
| 2 | **Tours & experiences affiliates** (GetYourGuide, Viator, trek operators) | Month 3–6 | High basket (multi-day treks US$500–3,000) → often beats gear |
| 3 | **Sponsorships** (brands/tourism boards: sponsored collections, Instagram features) | When IG ≥ 10k or site ≥ 10k visits/month | Always labelled; never inside rankings |
| 4 | **Display ads** (Ezoic → Journey by Mediavine at ~10k sessions) | Month 9–12 | Hurts speed/design — only if it pays clearly |
| 5 | **Merch** (print-on-demand) | Later | Brand play more than income |
| 6 | **Premium** (offline packs, AI planner) | Later | Only if community asks |

## Simple traffic → income model (month 12)
| Scenario | Monthly visitors | Shop/partner click rate | Orders (2 % of clicks) | Avg order | Commission | ≈ Monthly income |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Low | 3,000 | 4 % (120 clicks) | 2–3 | US$120 | 7 % | **~US$20** |
| Base | 10,000 | 5 % (500) | 10 | US$150 | 8 % | **~US$120** + tours ~US$100 = **~US$220** |
| High | 30,000 | 6 % (1,800) | 36 | US$150 | 8 % | **~US$430** + tours ~US$300 + ads ~US$200 = **~US$930** |

Takeaway: income follows traffic; tours and guides that convert matter more than a bigger catalogue.

## Costs (monthly)
| Phase | Items | ≈ US$/month |
|---|---|---|
| **Phase 0 — now** | Domain (~US$12/yr), Supabase free, Resend free, Behold free, Unsplash free | **~US$1** |
| **Phase 0 must-fix** | **Vercel Hobby is for non-commercial use only** — once affiliate links earn money, move to **Vercel Pro (US$20/month)** | **US$20** |
| Optional | Anthropic API for the AI finder (cap US$10), Behold paid plan (more IG posts) | US$10–25 |
| Marketing test | Ads test (approved, one-off) | US$150 once |
| Phase 1 — when income ≥ costs | More ads budget, Supabase Pro if limits hit (US$25), newsletter tool | scale with results |

**Rule:** only raise spending when the previous step paid back or proved its metric (e.g. ads only
scale if cost per engaged visitor < target set in B3).

## Unit metrics to track
Revenue per 1,000 visitors (RPM), click-out rate per page type, conversion per partner, cost per
member, cost per engaged visitor (ads), share of traffic from Google vs Instagram.
