# Instagram content policy (owner decision 2026-10-08)

## The rules
1. **We don't show the #great_hikes hashtag on the website.** Anyone can use a hashtag; it is not
   permission and not curation. The old `/community` page (hashtag feed) now redirects to
   **Our feed**. The hashtag feed is kept only privately, to help the owner *discover* photos.
2. **Only features the owner posts on @great_hikes appear on the site** — curated, credited,
   with the photographer's permission (constitution III; consent template in
   `docs/marketing/instagram-warmup.md`). They show in **Our feed**, **From our community**
   (landing), and the hike's **gallery / "Featured on @great_hikes"** section, if the owner says so.
3. **Instagram images are low-resolution (≈1080 px)** — never a hike's main (hero) photo. They go
   into **galleries, community cards and journal articles**. Every hike's main photo must be
   high-resolution (≥ 2000 px) from a licensed source (owner's own photos, Unsplash, Wikimedia
   Commons with a free licence, NPS public domain, or a photographer's full-size original sent with
   permission).
4. **A feature can seed new content:** from a selected Instagram post the team may create a **hike
   page** (researched from official sources, high-res main photo) or a **journal story**, and
   feature the Instagram image inside it with credit.
5. **Owner-first content to restart the community.** Few people tag @great_hikes today (account
   dormant since ~2020), and Instagram (since 30 Apr 2026) recommends *original* content, not
   reposts. So the warm-up leads with the owner's own photos, trips and planning content (Permit
   Watch, carousels with facts and maps), and adds Collab features with photographers once
   engagement returns.

## What changed on the site (2026-10-08)
- `/community` → permanent redirect to `/our-feed`; removed from the footer and sitemap.
- The add-hike workflow and the Instagram→hike sync no longer use an Instagram image as the main
  photo.
- To fix in the next content batch: 4 hikes still use an Instagram image as their main photo —
  Refugio Laguna Negra, Mount Hood National Forest, Zion National Park, Jumbo Pass → give each a
  high-res main photo; keep the Instagram post in its gallery/"Featured" section.

## Why
- **Trust & law:** only content we have permission for (red lines, B6).
- **Quality:** the site's identity is full-bleed photography; low-res heroes look broken on large
  screens.
- **Growth:** original content is what Instagram distributes now; features then reward the
  community and bring tags back.
