# Great Hikes — strategy (B1 draft v1, 2026-10-08)

**Status:** draft for the owner's review. Inputs: owner answers (2026-10-08), `docs/discovery/01–05`.

## 1. What kind of business this is
A **side project with financial return**, run by one person (~2 h a day, confirmed 2026-10-08), with a small starting budget that grows only when results show up ("invest small,
scale on evidence"). Everything below is sized for that: low fixed costs, automation by agents,
the owner approving rather than producing.

## 2. Vision & mission
- **Mission:** help people find and plan the world's great hikes — told and photographed by the
  hikers who walked them.
- **3-year vision:** the go-to place to *choose* a hike (inspiration + trip facts), with a
  community that gets credited and featured, in English for the US and Europe, then Portuguese,
  Spanish and French.

## 3. Positioning
> For hikers planning their next great trip, **Great Hikes** is the community guide that shows
> the world's best hikes through credited photos from real hikers, with the facts to go — unlike
> AllTrails or Komoot, which are navigation databases, and unlike blogs, which are one person's
> trip.

**We are:** inspiration + planning (where, when, how hard, permits, getting there), photo-first,
community-credited. **We are not:** a GPS/navigation app (we link to AllTrails/Komoot/Gaia for
that), a tour operator, a gear review lab.

## 4. Audiences
| Segment | Who | Job to be done | How we reach them |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Trip planners (primary, US)** | 25–45, plan 1–3 big hiking trips a year (national parks, Patagonia, Alps, Nepal) | "Which hike, when, how hard, what do I need?" | SEO (guides, hike pages), Pinterest, Instagram |
| **Community photographers** | Instagram hikers who love being featured | "Get my photo seen and credited" | @great_hikes features, #great_hikes, "Share your hike" |
| **Partners** | Outdoor brands, retailers, tour operators, tourism boards | Reach engaged hikers | Affiliates now; sponsorships later |

## 5. Markets & sequence
1. **US (English)** — launch market: largest affiliate programmes (AvantLink), the owner's
   partner is American (local insight), national parks content.
2. **Europe in English** (UK/IE + travellers to the Alps, Dolomites, Scotland) — same site.
3. **Localised relaunches** (spec 012): **Brazil (PT)** — owner is Brazilian (native language,
   local network), Spain (ES), France (FR); then Greece, Turkey, Thailand.

## 6. Advantages to build on
- 4k-follower Instagram collective with a credit culture (people *want* to be featured).
- Owner's design skills (identity, UX) and agents that produce sourced content cheaply.
- Trilingual/multicultural owner (EN/PT/ES) for the relaunches.

## 7. Red lines (approved 2026-10-08 — constitution VII, `docs/business/red-lines.md`)
No explicit content; never use someone's photo without permission and credit; no paid placement
inside guides or rankings; no fake or paid reviews; no AI images presented as real; no selling
personal data; no promotion of unsafe/illegal behaviour; clear labels on anything sponsored.

## 8. Year-one goals (OKRs, base case — agreed 2026-10-08)
| Goal | Key results by month 12 |
|---|---|
| **Community growth** | Instagram 4k → 10k followers · 1,000 registered members · 30 approved community photos/month · 50 reviews/tips per month |
| **Income** | First affiliate commission by month 4 · US$100–300/month by month 12 · costs covered from month 9 |
| **Personal brand** | "Run by Yuri Cooke" visible (About, launch video) · 3 partner collaborations · 1 press/podcast mention |
| **Product/content** | 100 hikes, 50 guides, 200 trails · Core Web Vitals "good" · 0 unresolved rights complaints |

Measured with Vercel Analytics, Supabase counts, Instagram insights, AvantLink reports; reviewed
monthly in `docs/discovery/learning-log.md`.

## 9. Brand decisions pending
Domain (`greathikes.app`/`.co`/…), trademark search for "Great Hikes" (US/EU) before spending on
brand, tone of voice ("experienced friend, practical, never preachy").

## 10. Main risks & mitigations
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Instagram/Meta dependency | Own the audience: accounts, newsletter, SEO |
| AI search answering trip questions | Be the cited source (official facts, freshness, credited photos); AI finder on-site (013) |
| Affiliate rejections | Trust pages (done), quality content first, apply in the right order |
| Content liability (wrong/outdated info) | Sources + "checked on" dates, recheck cycles (B5), disclaimers (B6) |
| Owner time | Agents draft, owner approves; batch work weekly |
