# Ads test plan (B3, budget US$150 — approved 2026-10-07)

**Goal:** learn which audience + message brings *engaged* US hikers at a cost we can scale — not
to "grow fast". Runs 3 weeks after launch (mid-Jan → early Feb 2027, peak planning season).

## Split
| Channel | Budget | What | Audience |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram (Meta Ads via the Great Hikes Business Portfolio) | US$90 | Boost the 2 best-performing organic reels/carousels → hike page or guide | US, 25–45, interests: hiking, backpacking, national parks, REI/Patagonia-style brands |
| Pinterest Ads | US$40 | Promote 3 pins: "Plan the W Trek", "Half Dome permits 2027", "Best day hikes in Yosemite" | US, keywords: hiking trips, national park itinerary |
| Reserve | US$20 | Double down on the winner in week 3 | — |

(Reddit Ads is an alternative to Pinterest if the owner prefers.)

## What we measure
- **Cost per engaged visitor** (≥ 2 pages or ≥ 45 s) — target **< US$0.40**
- **Cost per follower** (Instagram) — target **< US$0.30**
- **Cost per member** (sign-up) — target **< US$3**
- Click-out to partners from ad traffic (directional only).

## Rules
- Run each ad ≥ 4 days or 1,000 impressions before judging.
- **Stop** an ad above 2× the target cost; **scale** (next phase) only if a channel beats targets
  and the visitors behave like organic ones.
- Credited community photos only (consent covers promotion — confirm in the DM wording), labelled
  as ads by the platform; follow red lines (constitution VII).
- Log results in `docs/discovery/learning-log.md`; decide Phase 1 budget from them.
