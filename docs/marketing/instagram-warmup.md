**One-line summary:** the old @great_hikes format (repost a photo + "Place | @photographer") is

## 1. What Instagram rewards in 2025–2026

### 1.1 Ranking signals
- Mosseri named **watch time, likes and sends (DM shares)** as the top three ranking signals;
  likes weigh more with followers, **sends weigh more with non-followers** — i.e. sends drive
  discovery ([Social Samosa](https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/instagram-reveals-key-factors-for-boosting-reach-8646916),
  [Inman, Mar 2025](https://news.inman.com/2025/03/07/head-of-instagram-reveals-secrets-to-social-media-success/)).
  The "sends are 3–5× likes" figure circulating in blogs is **(unverified)**.
- Instagram's official explainer: each surface (Feed, Stories, Reels, Explore) has its own
  ranking; predictions include how likely you are to like, comment, share, save or tap the profile
  ([Instagram ranking explained](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained)).

### 1.2 Formats
| Format | What's known | Use for us |
|---|---|---|
| **Reels** | Main route to non-followers (Reels tab, Explore). Trial reels let you show a reel **only to non-followers** first; after 24 h you see views/likes/shares and can share to followers, or auto-share if it performs in the first 72 h ([Instagram Creators: Trial reels](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels)). | 1/week from permitted footage; use Trial for every new idea while the follower base is cold. |
| **Carousels** | Mosseri: carousels get more reach than single photos because more slides = more interactions, and an unswiped carousel is shown again on the next slide; up to 20 slides; carousels with music can appear in Reels ([Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/ig-chief-recommends-posting-carousels-to-improve-reach/730232/)). | Default for features and Permit Watch: photo + facts + map slides. |
| **Single photos** | Lowest reach of the three on average (same source). | Rare; only with a strong standalone photo. |
| **Stories** | Reach mostly existing followers; polls/questions/stickers build interaction history that helps later feed ranking (ranking explainer above). | Daily-ish light touch: polls, "Featured on Great Hikes" shares. |

### 1.3 Originality — the big risk for a feature account
- **2024 (Reels):** Instagram began replacing reposts with originals in recommendations and
  stopped recommending accounts that mainly repost others' reels.
- **30 Apr 2026 (photos + carousels):** the same rule now covers photos and carousels. Accounts that
  *primarily* post unoriginal content are **not eligible for recommendations** (Explore, Reels,
  Feed suggestions, Suggested accounts); followers still see posts
  ([Instagram Creators: Rewarding original creators](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/rewarding-original-creators-on-instagram),
  [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/30/instagram-restricts-reach-of-content-aggregators-in-new-crackdown/),
  [Tubefilter](https://tubefilter.com/2026/04/30/instagram-removes-algorithm-recommendations-repost-content-aggregator)).
- **Original** = you created it, or you **materially transformed** third-party content (unique
  text, edits, voiceover, commentary, a real perspective). **Not enough:** borders, watermarks,
  speed changes, a caption credit or a screenshot credit. Instagram says **credit alone does not
  make content original** and you are responsible for getting permission first (Instagram post above).
- **Recovery:** an account becomes eligible again when **most of its recently posted photos,
  carousels and reels in a rolling 30-day window are original**; check standing in
  **Settings → Account Status → Recommendations** and appeal there
  ([Instagram Help: Account Status](https://help.instagram.com/539126347315373),
  [Instagram Creators: recommendations eligibility](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-recommendations-eligibility-tips-creators)).
  Old 2016–2020 posts are outside the window — no need to delete them.
- Instagram points to **Collab, Remix and paid-partnership labels** as the right way to credit
  co-creators (Instagram post above). Whether a Collab post with the photographer *by itself*
  counts as original for the account that posts it is **(unverified)** — so we always add our own
  value too (see §2).

### 1.4 Credit and sharing tools
- **Collab posts:** one post on both profiles, shared likes/comments/views; up to 5 collaborators;
  the invite must be sent when publishing and the photographer must accept
  ([Hootsuite](https://blog.hootsuite.com/instagram-collab-post/), [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-collaborative-posts)) **(details secondary-sourced)**.
  It puts our post in front of the photographer's followers — the best warm-up lever we have.
- **Repost button (Aug 2025):** reposts others' public posts/reels into a "Reposts" tab, always
  credited to the creator ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/how-to-repost-on-instagram/)).
  Use this (and Stories) for plain shares that we don't transform — they don't sit in our grid as
  our own posts.
- **Tags** (photo tag + location tag) still help the photographer get notified and the post get
  categorised; tag on every post.

### 1.5 Hashtags
Mosseri: hashtags help **search/categorisation**, not reach. Instagram capped hashtags at **5 per
post** from Dec 2025 ([Lilach Bullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/why-instagram-only-lets-you-use-5-hashtags-some-posts/),
[My Mobile India](https://www.mymobileindia.com/instagram-limits-hashtags-to-five-per-post-and-reel-heres-key-changes-creators-should-know/)) **(cap secondary-sourced)**.
Use 3–5 specific tags: `#great_hikes` + place + park/trail (e.g. `#yosemite #halfdome`).
Keywords in the caption and on-image text matter more for Instagram search.

## 2. Risks for @great_hikes and how we stay "original"

| Risk | Why | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| **Ineligible for recommendations** | A grid of credited reposts = aggregator under the 2026 rule | ≥ 70% of feed posts in any 30 days must be clearly ours (rule of thumb, not an Instagram number): Collab + added slides, Permit Watch, owner's own photos/footage, explainer reels |
| **Cold follower base** | 6 years silent; many followers inactive → first posts get weak engagement, which ranks later posts lower | Start with Stories + Trial reels; Collab posts borrow warm audiences; don't buy or "follow/unfollow" |
| **Rights complaints** | Reposting without consent breaks red line 2 and Instagram's IP rules | Written DM consent before posting, logged (`ugc-curation` skill); takedown in 48 h |
| **Consent too narrow** | Ads test (Jan) and Pinterest pins need permission for those uses too | DM template below asks separately for site, Pinterest and paid promotion |
| **Wrong trip facts** | Facts in captions/slides are part of "added value" | Official sources only, "checked on" date (red line 6) |
| **Owner time** | 4 h/week only | Batch on Tue/Thu (gtm-plan §6); agents draft captions and fact slides; owner approves |

**How each post becomes "ours":**
1. **Collab with the photographer** (they co-author; credit is structural, not a caption line).
2. **Added slides:** distance, gain, best months, permit, getting there, a simple map — designed in
   our identity (dark pill labels, glass panels). This is our "unique text / edit".
3. **Owner's own content:** owner's hikes, behind-the-scenes of the rebuild, explainer reels with
   our voiceover/text.
4. **Series formats** nobody else does: **Permit Watch**, **Hike of the Week**, "Plan your 2027".
5. Plain reshares only via **Stories / Repost button**, never as grid posts.
6. Never: AI images, AI-altered photos (red line 3), unsafe spots (red line 4), exact sensitive
   locations (red line 5).

## 5. Templates

### A. Consent DM (EN)
> Hi @handle! I run @great_hikes, a community of hikers that's relaunching as a website with
> trip guides. Your photo of **[place]** is stunning and we'd love to feature it.
>
> If you're happy with it, we would:
> 1. Post it on Instagram as a **Collab post** with you (it shows on both profiles), with
>    trip facts we add (distance, best season, permits).
> 2. Add it to the **[place] page on great-hikes.vercel.app** with your name and a link to your profile.
>
> Optional — tell us yes/no to each:
> 3. Pin it on our **Pinterest**, credited to you.
> 4. Use it in a **paid promotion** of that post on Instagram/Pinterest (always credited).
>
> We never edit your photo beyond resizing/cropping, never use AI on it, and you can ask us to
> remove it anytime. Can we feature it? A simple "yes to 1–2" (or "yes to all") is perfect.
> Thanks! — Yuri, Great Hikes

*Part 2 (after publishing):* "It's live: [link]. Here's a 'Featured on Great Hikes' card if you'd
like to share it in your stories. Thanks for letting us show your work!"

Record the reply (screenshot + entry in the consent log) before posting — `ugc-curation` skill.

### B. Caption template (feature Collab)
```
[Place], [Park/Region] | 📷 @photographer      ← credit in the first line, always

[1 sentence hook with a real fact: "The last 400 ft are on cables — and you need a permit."]

↔ [distance] mi · ⬆ [gain] ft · ⏱ [time] · Best: [months]
🎟 Permit: [yes/no + how, "checked on DD Mon YYYY", source: NPS]
📍 Getting there: [1 line]

Planning it? Full guide + map → link in bio ([page name]).
Save this for your 2027 list and send it to who you'd hike it with.

Want to be featured? Tag #great_hikes. We always ask first and credit you.
#great_hikes #[place] #[park] #[region]hiking            ← max 5
```
Alt text: describe the photo (accessibility, constitution). No emojis on the image itself.

### C. "Permit Watch" carousel (7–9 slides, 4:5)
1. **Cover:** credited photo (Collab) + title "Permit Watch · [Month YYYY]" + "Save this".
2. **What's opening this month:** list of 3–5 permits with dates (one line each).
3–6. **One slide per permit:** name · where · what opens (lottery / first-come / quota) ·
   window dates · cost · what to have ready · "Source: [agency], checked [date]".
7. **Deadlines calendar:** mini timeline of the next 3 months.
8. **Tip slide:** one practical tip (e.g. "set an alarm 10 min before release; log in early").
9. **CTA:** "Full 2027 permit calendar → link in bio · Send to your hiking buddy · Photo: @handle".

Caption: summary + official links listed + "checked on" date + credit. Correct publicly within
24 h if a date changes (red line 6). Reel version: 20–30 s, text on screen, voiceover optional.
