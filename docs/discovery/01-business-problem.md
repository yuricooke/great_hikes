# 01 · Business Problem

Status: draft for owner review · 2026-10-07

## Context

@great_hikes is an Instagram collective (~2.9k followers per the Behold feed, 2026-10-07) that
has featured hikers' photos since ~2017, credited as "Place | @photographer". The website
(great-hikes.vercel.app) was a 2023 course MVP with 32 static hikes and no connection to the
community.

## Problem statement

Hikers share great photos and experiences, but:

1. **For hikers/photographers:** being featured on Instagram is fleeting — a post scrolls away in
   a day, adds no lasting credit, and says nothing practical about the trail.
2. **For people planning a hike:** inspiration (Instagram) and practical trail info (distance,
   elevation, season, conditions — scattered across AllTrails, park sites, blogs) live in
   different places.
3. **For Great Hikes:** the community lives entirely on a platform it doesn't control (algorithm
   reach, account risk — already experienced with Facebook), with no owned audience, no
   searchable content, and no revenue.

## Why now

- The owner wants to relaunch "in the near future".
- The Instagram import is technically proven (Behold feed, 2026-10-07) without Meta developer
  access.
- Six-plus years of curated, credited posts already exist — a content base most new sites lack.

## Who has the problem

| Segment | Pain |
|---------|------|
| Hikers who post on Instagram | Want recognition and lasting credit for their photos |
| Hike planners (inspiration-driven) | Find a beautiful place, then struggle to learn if/how they can hike it |
| Great Hikes (owner) | No owned channel, no durable content, no way to monetize the community |

## What is out of scope (for now)

- Navigation/GPS tracking apps (AllTrails, Komoot, Gaia do this well).
- Guided tours / booking.
- Social network features beyond featuring, reviews and tips.

## Owner decisions (2026-10-07)

- **Year-one goals: all three** — community growth, income, and personal brand. Growth will be
  driven by paid ads and marketing with a planned investment and expected return.
- **Launch date: none yet.** Launch happens when the strategy is complete on both fronts: product
  (code) and marketing (go-to-market).
- **Owner time: approve only.** Curation must be automated — agents find, prepare and propose;
  the owner approves or rejects.
