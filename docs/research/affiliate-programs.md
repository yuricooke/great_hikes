**Networks**
- **AvantLink** is the outdoor specialist: most hiking brands and retailers above are on it. You apply to each brand separately, and a brand that rejects you will not let you apply again ([AvantLink support](https://support.avantlink.com/hc/en-us/articles/203883345-All-About-Datafeeds)). It has daily-updated datafeeds with image URL, price and buy link, plus a REST API with Product Search ([API docs](https://support.avantlink.com/hc/en-us/articles/203644699-Affiliate-API-Technical-Integration)).
- **Impact / CJ** have more brands but are pickier. They tend to favour sites with a clear traffic story; 1k–10k monthly visitors is a common rule of thumb, not a hard rule ([Post Affiliate Pro](https://www.postaffiliatepro.com/faq/website-traffic-affiliate-network-acceptance/), [AffNinja](https://affninja.com/impact-affiliate-review/)).
- **Awin (which now includes ShareASale)** charges a refundable €/£/$1 sign-up deposit ([Awin](https://success.awin.com/articles/en_US/Knowledge/Why-do-I-have-to-pay-the-sign-up-fee)). It is the strongest network for the EU.
- **Rakuten Advertising** also covers Brazil ([Divulga Ninja](https://www.divulganinja.com.br/en/blog/rakuten-awin-affiliate-networks-10-faq-mistakes)).

## 3. Rules

**FTC (US)**
- Affiliate relationships must be disclosed "clearly and conspicuously," on the same page as the link, and without needing a click, hover or scroll to see it.
- "Affiliate link" on its own, or a "Buy now" button, is **not** enough. "Paid link" next to the link, or a plain sentence near the top of the page ("We earn a commission if you buy through links on this page"), is.
- The same applies to Instagram posts and stories.

Sources: [FTC FAQs](https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/documents/plain-language/pdf-0205-endorsement-guides-faqs_0.pdf), [Manatt](https://manatt.com/insights/newsletters/advertising-law/an-in-depth-look-at-the-ftcs-updates-2).

**EU (Spain, France, Greece)**
- Under the Unfair Commercial Practices Directive, publishers who earn commissions count as "traders." Not disclosing affiliate links can be a misleading practice ([GT Law](https://www.gtlaw.com/en/insights/2025/4/influencer-marketing-practices-under-scrutiny-in-europe)).
- **France** (Law 2023-451) requires "Publicité" or "Collaboration commerciale" labels, using platform tools where they exist ([economie.gouv.fr](https://www.economie.gouv.fr/node/3222759), [Swello](https://swello.com/fr/blog/loi-influenceur/)).

**Brazil**
- CONAR's influencer guide (in force since June 2026) explicitly covers commissions and affiliate models.
- Use "#publi" or "#publicidade," visible without tapping "more" ([KLA Law](https://www.klalaw.com.br/conar-atualiza-guia-de-publicidade-com-influenciadores/), [Propmark](https://propmark.com.br/digital/conar-atualiza-guia-de-publicidade-com-influenciadores-e-inclui-regras-sobre-ia/)).

**Turkey**
- The Ministry of Trade influencer guideline (2021) bans hidden advertising and requires clear labels such as "#reklam" ([ticaret.gov.tr](https://ticaret.gov.tr/haberler/ticaret-bakanligi-sosyal-medya-etkileyicileri-icin-kilavuz-yayimladi)).

**Thailand**
- No affiliate-specific rule was verified. Apply the same clear disclosure anyway.

**Images and prices**
- **Amazon:** images and prices must come only from the Creators API / PA-API (or SiteStripe, which gives no images). Every image must link to Amazon. If prices are refreshed less often than hourly, show a date/time stamp next to them ([Conduct Atlas summary of Amazon policies](https://conductatlas.com/platform/amazon-associates/amazon-associates-program-policies/price-display-via-data-feeds-requires-timestamp-disclosure/), [AzonPress](https://azonpress.com/can-i-use-amazon-product-images-on-my-affiliate-website)). Until the 3-sale threshold is met, use text links only.
- **AvantLink / Awin / CJ / Impact:** use the merchant's **datafeed image URLs and prices**, which are licensed for affiliate use under program terms. Refresh them daily, because feeds update daily ([AvantLink](https://support.avantlink.com/hc/en-us/articles/203883345-All-About-Datafeeds)).
- Do not scrape or hotlink brand-site images.
- Never show stale prices; prefer "Check price" buttons when prices are not fresh.
- The site's own photos of the owner's gear are always safe to use.

## 4. Recommended plan

**Phase 1: US launch (apply in this order)**
1. **AvantLink account, then Backcountry, Osprey and Black Diamond.** These are hiking-core, pay 7–8% with 30-day cookies, have good feeds and in-house managers. AvantLink is the best fit for niche content sites.
2. **Patagonia (AvantLink US).** The owner's favourite brand, 8% with a 60-day cookie. Apply once there are 10–20 quality guide pages, because a rejection blocks reapplying.
3. **Garage Grown Gear and CampSaver (AvantLink).** These are smaller retailers that are more likely to accept a new site, and they cover the ultralight gear hikers search for.
4. **Amazon Associates (US).** Easy approval and broad catalogue, but only 3% with a 24-hour cookie. Use it as a fallback for generic items (socks, headlamps, poles). Use text links until 3 sales unlock the API.
5. **Later (once traffic is shown):** Arc'teryx and Columbia on CJ/Impact, Salomon, Garmin.

**Phase 2: per-country shortlist**
- **Spain:** Awin, then Decathlon ES and Bergfreunde/Alpinetrek ES; Amazon.es; Trekkinn; Patagonia EMEA (Impact).
- **France:** Awin, then Decathlon FR; Amazon.fr; Trekkinn FR; Patagonia EMEA. Ask Snowleader directly.
- **Greece:** Linkwise; Trekkinn; Amazon.de as a fallback. This is the biggest gap.
- **Brazil:** Lomadee (Decathlon BR, Centauro); Amazon.com.br.
- **Turkey:** Trendyol affiliate; Amazon.com.tr (request access). Watch Decathlon TR.
- **Thailand:** Involve Asia, for Shopee TH, Lazada TH and Decathlon. There is no Amazon.

**Before applying anywhere:**
- An About page.
- An affiliate disclosure page plus inline per-page disclosures.
- A privacy policy.
- 10+ original trail guides.
- Instagram linked as proof of audience.
