# Top 100 hikes worldwide (content plan input)

Checked 2026-10-08. Purpose: decide which 50+ hikes and 50 guides to publish before launch
(mid-January 2027). Related: `docs/discovery/06-competitors-v2.md` §3 (SEO landscape),
`docs/marketing/content-calendar.md`.

## Method and limits

- **No global ranking exists.** The rank is a judgement call that combines three signals:
  1. **Trail-level counts or caps** where published (permits, booking caps, trail counters,
     pilgrim certificates).
  2. **Park visitation** where no trail figure exists (NPS 2025 recreation visits, CONAF, ACAP).
  3. **Planning demand**: how often the hike shows up as a trip people book or apply for (permits,
     lotteries, operator listings, search results in our B2 sample).
- **The figures do not compare like for like.** A permit cap (Half Dome 300/day), a park total
  (Zion 4.98M) and a pilgrim count (Camino 242k) measure different things. Read them as "how
  big is the demand" signals, not as a league table.
- **No keyword volume data.** Semrush/Ahrefs volumes are paywalled. The "target search phrase"
  column in the guide list is our judgement, based on the B2 SERP sample. **Check volumes in
  Google Keyword Planner or Semrush before committing.**
- Lengths and durations are the usual official or guidebook values, rounded. RT = round trip.
- **(unverified)** = from a secondary source (blog, operator, news) or the figures conflict.
  "—" = no official figure found.
- Regions: US 35, Europe 25, Latin America 12, rest of world 28 (Canada, Asia, Oceania, Africa).
