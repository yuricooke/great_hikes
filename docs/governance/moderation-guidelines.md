# Moderation guidelines (B5, v1 2026-10-08)

Tools: `/moderation` (shared photos), Hide/Show/Delete on reviews & tips (owner account).

## Service levels
- Shared photos/hikes: review within **72 h**.
- Rights/takedown claims and safety-critical errors: **48 h** (photos removed first, then checked).
- Reported reviews/tips: 72 h.

## Approve a shared photo when
It's a real photo of the stated place (or close), the hiker confirmed ownership/consent, it's not
edited beyond normal photo adjustments, no faces of minors without consent, no private
information, and it respects the red lines (safe behaviour, sensitive places).

## Hide a review or tip when
Spam/ads or affiliate links; abuse, harassment or hate; private information; dangerous advice
(e.g. "skip the permit", "go off-trail where it's closed"); clearly fake or off-topic; copied text.
**Never hide** honest negative experiences that follow the rules.

## Decisions log
Record hide/reject decisions and the reason (moderation log table — to build), so patterns and
appeals can be handled.

## Appeals
Members can reply through /contact (topic "My data or account"); the owner reviews within 7 days.
