# Content policy (B5, v1 2026-10-08)

Applies to everything published on Great Hikes and @great_hikes. Binding rules: constitution
(III consent & attribution, VII red lines — `docs/business/red-lines.md`).

## 1. Who makes what
| Content | Drafted by | Checked by | Published by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hike & trail facts | Research agents (add-hike skill, trail importer) | Agent self-check + owner spot-check | Owner (`status: published`) |
| Journal guides | journal-writer agent | Owner reads in full | Owner |
| Photos (stock/Commons) | Photo scripts suggest | Owner picks | Script applies with credit |
| Community photos/hikes | Hikers (/share) | Owner in /moderation | Owner approves |
| Reviews & tips | Signed-in members | Published immediately; owner moderates after | — |
| Instagram features | Owner/agents prepare | Photographer consent by DM | Owner |

Status labels: `draft → published`, plus **needs recheck** (stale facts) and **hidden** (UGC).

## 2. Fact-checking rules
1. Trip facts only from **official/authoritative sources** (park authorities, government, official
   operators); each fact traceable in `sources` with a URL.
2. Conflicting sources → use the most official, note the conflict in the draft report, or leave out.
3. Unknown → `null`, never estimated numbers presented as fact. Map measurements are labelled.
4. Every hike/trail shows "checked on <date>"; guides show their date and sources.
5. Corrections: fix within 72 h of a valid report; log in `docs/governance/corrections-log.md`.

## 3. Freshness (recheck cycle)
| Content | Recheck |
|---|---|
| Permits, fees, booking windows, shuttles, road rules | Every season (before Jan, Apr, Jul, Oct) |
| Closures/temporary notices | Shown as **dated alerts with an expiry**; rechecked monthly |
| Distances, elevation, routes | Yearly |
| Guides | Yearly or when a rule changes; title year updated ("… 2027") |
| Photos/credits/licences | When a photo is replaced; licence record kept |

A monthly **stale-content report** lists items older than their cycle (script to build).

## 4. AI-assisted content
- Agents draft; a human reviews and approves every page (disclosed on /about).
- No AI-generated or AI-edited images, ever (constitution VII).
- Agents must cite sources and report dropped/unverified facts; the owner reads that report.

## 5. Photos
- Allowed: own photos; community photos with recorded consent; Unsplash/Pexels within licence;
  Wikimedia Commons with free licences (public domain, CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA) — credited with
  licence link; NPS/US government public domain.
- Credit format: "Photo by X on Unsplash/Pexels" · "Photo: X · CC BY-SA 4.0" · "Photo: @handle".
- Only resize/re-encode; colour look is a CSS filter, not an edit to the file.
- Alt text describes the place and the view (not "landscape photo").
- Remove on request within 48 h.

## 6. Writing style
Plain, friendly, practical; second person for advice; no first-person trip stories by the team
(those come from hikers); metric + imperial; safety line pointing to official conditions in every
guide; no hype words without specifics.
