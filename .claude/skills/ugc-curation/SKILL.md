# Curating community content

Constitution Principle III is non-negotiable: **a tag or mention is not permission.**
Owner rule: **no identifiable @photographer → the post is never featured.**

## Accepted permission evidence (one is required)
- On-site submission where the contributor accepted the Great Hikes contributor license.
- Instagram DM opt-in: the creator replied "yes" to a consent request sent by DM from
  @great_hikes — store a screenshot/link of the reply and the date. (No consent hashtag.)
- Written permission by email/DM — store a copy.

## Record for every featured item
`source` (site | instagram | email), `creatorName`, `creatorHandle`, `creatorUrl`,
`permissionEvidence` (link/file), `permissionDate`, `licenseScope` (site + social + shop?),
`hikeId`, `status` (pending | approved | featured | rejected | removed), `moderatedBy`.

## Moderation checklist
- [ ] Permission evidence present and matches the creator.
- [ ] Content relates to a hike/region on the site (or proposes a new one).
- [ ] No personal data, faces of minors without consent, illegal/unsafe behavior (off-trail in
      protected areas, drones where banned, wildlife harassment).
- [ ] Location precision is appropriate (no exact sensitive sites, e.g. nesting areas).
- [ ] Credit renders visibly with a link to the creator.
- [ ] Takedown is possible: item can be unpublished by id.

## After featuring
Suggest a thank-you message to the creator (comment/DM) linking to their featured page — this
is the growth loop. Never send messages on the owner's behalf without explicit approval.
