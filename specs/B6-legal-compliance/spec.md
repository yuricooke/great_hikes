# B6 — Legal & compliance

**Status:** to do (owner request 2026-10-08). Current Terms/Privacy (spec 004) are plain-language
templates — **not legal advice**; this spec gets them reviewed and complete before launch.

## Scope
1. **Who is the operator** — individual vs company (Spain-based owner? US market): entity,
   address for notices, tax for affiliate income; contact for legal requests.
2. **Terms of use** — liability for hiking information (risk disclaimer), UGC licence (photos,
   text, facts; right to edit — spec 010b), acceptable use, account termination, governing law &
   jurisdiction, dispute resolution.
3. **Privacy** — GDPR (EU owner/visitors), CCPA/CPRA (US), LGPD (Brazil, relaunch), PDPA (Thailand),
   KVKK (Turkey) — data map (Supabase, Vercel, Google, MET Norway, OpenStreetMap, Unsplash,
   Wikimedia), processors & DPAs, retention, user rights flow (export/delete account), breach
   procedure, children.
4. **Cookies & tracking** — current: no tracking cookies (Vercel Analytics cookieless); rules if
   ads/affiliates add cookies (consent banner by region).
5. **Copyright** — DMCA agent/notice & takedown procedure (US), EU equivalent; image licence
   records (Unsplash/Commons/permissions) kept with each photo; Instagram embedding exception
   (constitution).
6. **Advertising & affiliates** — FTC endorsement guides (US), EU UCPD, France influencer law,
   Brazil CONAR, Turkey ad labelling; "Sponsored"/"Partner" labels; Amazon/AvantLink programme
   terms.
7. **Trademarks** — "Great Hikes" name/logo search & registration (US/EU) before spending on brand.
8. **Accessibility law** — ADA (US) / European Accessibility Act (2025) exposure.
9. **Platform terms** — Instagram/Meta, Behold, Unsplash API, Google OAuth, OpenStreetMap ODbL,
   MET Norway licence, Supabase/Vercel.

## Deliverables
Reviewed Terms + Privacy (+ cookie policy if needed), DMCA page, legal register
(`docs/legal/register.md`: obligations, owner, review date), checklist per relaunch country.
**Needs a lawyer** for final wording and entity/tax questions.
