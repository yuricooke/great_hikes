# B1 — Business strategy & model

**Status:** to do (owner request 2026-10-08). Builds on `docs/discovery/01–04`.

## Questions this spec answers
1. **Vision & mission** — what Great Hikes is in 3 years (community + guide + marketplace?).
2. **Positioning** — one sentence vs AllTrails/Komoot/blogs (draft in `docs/discovery/05-competitors.md`).
3. **Audiences** — primary (US hikers planning a bucket-list trip), secondary (photographers /
   Instagram community), partners (brands, tour operators, tourism boards).
4. **Value proposition** per audience (jobs-to-be-done, pains, gains).
5. **Business model** — revenue lines and their order: gear affiliates → tours/experiences
   affiliates → sponsorships → display ads → merch → premium (maps/offline/AI?) ; costs (hosting,
   APIs, content, ads); unit economics (revenue per 1,000 visitors, cost per member).
6. **Year-one goals & OKRs** — community growth, income, personal brand (owner's three goals) with
   measurable targets and the metrics loop (`04-outcomes-and-metrics.md`, `learning-log.md`).
7. **Brand** — name/domain decision, tone of voice, brand assets.
8. **Risks** — Meta/Instagram dependency, AI search, affiliate approvals, content liability.

## Deliverables
- `docs/business/strategy.md` (1–2 pages, lean canvas + positioning statement).
- `docs/business/model.md` (revenue lines, assumptions, 3 scenarios: low/base/high).
- OKRs table linked to analytics (Vercel Analytics, Supabase counts).
