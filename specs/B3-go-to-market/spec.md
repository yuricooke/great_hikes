# B3 — Go-to-market & growth

**Status:** draft v1 written 2026-10-08 — `docs/marketing/gtm-plan.md`, `ads-test-plan.md`,
`content-calendar.md` (owner to review). Replaces roadmap marketing items M1–M4.

## Scope
- **Launch plan** — pre-launch (domain, content minimum: 50 hikes / 25 guides?), launch week
  (video — `docs/marketing/launch-video-script.md`, Instagram, featured photographers), post-launch.
- **Channels** — Instagram (@great_hikes features, reels, stories), SEO (journal + hike pages),
  Pinterest (trip boards), newsletter, Reddit/communities (value-first), partnerships.
- **Content calendar** — weekly: 1 guide, 3 features, 1 new hike; seasonal (what to hike this month).
- **Community loop** — tag → feature → credit on the site → "Featured on Great Hikes" share card →
  more tags.
- **Paid test** — ads test up to US$150 (approved): audiences, creatives, budget split, success
  metrics (cost per engaged visitor / follower / sign-up), stop/scale rules.
- **Investment & return plan** — budget per phase, payback rules.

## Deliverables
`docs/marketing/gtm-plan.md`, `content-calendar.md`, `ads-test-plan.md`, KPIs dashboard
definition.
